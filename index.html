Cihangir
A bohemian hilltop neighbourhood beloved by artists, writers and expats. Known for antique shops, cosy cafés, and sweeping Bosphorus views.
Karaköy
A buzzing waterfront district at the foot of Galata Bridge — legendary pastry houses, artisan coffee, and a beautifully restored Ottoman hamam.
